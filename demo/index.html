glassity

Every icon below is a plain Lucide <svg data-glass>. One call to auto() turns them into glass.

Glass icons — regular

On a glass tile — press them

Groups — icons that come close join into one piece of glass

Light backdrop — the glass flips to dark by itself

Over real page content — no background image, the HTML itself is refracted

Glass does not blur what is behind it. It bends it. Lines that cross the edge of a lens break and reappear a little to one side, the way a straw seems to snap at the surface of a glass of water.

The icons floating over this text are ordinary SVG. Behind them is ordinary HTML: paragraphs, coloured blocks, a highlighted phrase. Nothing here was drawn on a canvas by hand.

When the content changes, the picture the glass looks through is taken again. Move the pointer near an icon and the highlight follows it.

Short strokes become round tubes of glass. Wide shapes get a flat top and a bevelled rim. Both come from the same icon outline, untouched.
